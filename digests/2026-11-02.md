# AI digest – 2026-11-02

## Tools

- [nemotron-halloween](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/nemotron-halloween)
  The article describes the creation of a haunted house project called MonsterHouse, which uses NVIDIA Nemotron Open Models to create an interactive projection. The project uses a combination of NVIDIA's AI technologies, including the Nemotron Voice Agent, Nemotron ASR, Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B, Magpie TTS Zeroshot, and Audio2Face-3D, to create a grumpy old pirate ship-like character that responds to visitor speech. The article provides a detailed overview of the project's architecture and the technologies used to bring the character to life.

## Business

- [Granite Switch 4.1.1 — Update models like you update software](https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-granite/granite-switch-4-1-1)
  The article announces the release of Granite Switch 4.1.1, a patch update that includes improvements to the speech-to-text model and decision adapter. The update is notable for its speed and efficiency, allowing developers to easily deploy new adapters without retraining the base model. The company plans to release tutorials and an Adapter SDK to enable third-party providers to build their own adapters and iterate on versions.

## Research

- [Announcing Project ProtoStar: Making Publicly Funded Science AI-Ready](https://huggingface.co/blog/announcing-project-protostar)
  Project ProtoStar is an initiative to make publicly funded scientific research more accessible and reusable for AI-driven research. It aims to unlock the potential of publicly funded research by releasing scientific artifacts in AI-ready formats and demonstrating their value through open challenges. The initiative brings together research institutions, industry, and the open science community to support the release of scientific artifacts in machine-actionable formats, and to facilitate the reuse and combination of scientific resources across disciplines.
